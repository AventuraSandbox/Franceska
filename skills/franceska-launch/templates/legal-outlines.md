# Legal page outlines

Starting points only. They are not legal advice and not finished policies. Have a lawyer review
them, or record in LEG-6 that the owner accepted the risk.

## Privacy policy: sections to cover
1. Who we are and how to contact us (a real address or email)
2. What we collect: account data, usage data, payment data (held by the processor), support messages
3. Why we collect it and the lawful basis (contract, legitimate interest, consent)
4. Who receives it: list every processor by name and purpose (host, database, payments, email,
   analytics, error tracking) and where they process it
5. Cookies and similar tech, and how consent works
6. How long we keep each kind of data
7. User rights: access, correction, deletion, export, objection; how to use them and how fast we reply
8. International transfers and the safeguard used
9. Children: the minimum age and what happens if we learn someone is younger
10. Security in plain words, and what we do after a breach
11. Changes to the policy and how users hear about them

## Terms of service: sections to cover
1. Who may use the service and the account rules
2. What the service is, and what it is not
3. Plans, prices, renewal, cancellation and refunds (match what the product actually does)
4. Acceptable use
5. Content users create: who owns it, what licence we need to run the service
6. Our IP, and no use of anyone else's
7. Availability, support and changes to the service
8. Termination by either side and what happens to data
9. Warranty disclaimers and liability limits (lawyer to set for your jurisdiction)
10. Governing law and disputes
11. How we contact you and how you contact us

## Also on the site
- Cookie banner if non-essential cookies apply (EU, UK)
- A data-request path (an email alias is fine) that actually gets answered
- Support contact and stated response time
